Our Security Services

Volk Security Systems offers services that can be installed separately or connected together.

1

Alarm Systems

Alarm panels and sensors help detect doors opening, movement, glass breaking and other security events.

  • Qolsys, DSC and Paradox options
  • Wireless and hardwired sensors
  • Mobile app arming and disarming
  • System upgrades and takeovers
2

Video Surveillance

Camera systems provide live viewing, recorded video and event notifications.

  • Indoor and outdoor cameras
  • NVR recording systems
  • Hikvision, UNV and Alarm.com options
  • Remote viewing from phones and computers
3

Professional Monitoring

Monitored alarm signals can be sent to a monitoring station for response.

  • 24-hour monitoring support
  • Alarm signal verification
  • Emergency contact procedures
  • Canadian monitoring through Lanvac
4

Access Control

Access-control systems help manage who can enter selected doors.

  • Cards, fobs and mobile credentials
  • Door schedules and user permissions
  • Entry activity records
  • Electric lock and door hardware integration
5

Smart Home Features

Compatible devices can be controlled through one mobile app.

  • Smart locks
  • Thermostats
  • Garage-door control
  • Lights and automation rules
6

Service and Support

Existing systems may need troubleshooting, replacement parts or programming changes.

  • Remote app support
  • Camera and recorder troubleshooting
  • Sensor replacement
  • System expansion and upgrades
